The optimized generation is content-addressed and never mixes scalar partitions. Materialize delivery-date PASA, half-hour weather and aggregated coal indexes; construct complete daily origin/lead grids with batch joins and vectorized feature algebra. Use exactly two Windows-spawned feature workers with one numerical thread each and coordinator-only ledger writes. Model execution uses at most two cell workers and two LightGBM threads per worker. Layer fingerprints by source/parser, prepared index, feature, model and report contracts so presentation or methodology-only revisions do not invalidate immutable sources. A soft batch cutoff stops new dispatch, lets active partitions finish and exits resumable rather than failed.

Correctness gates precede speed gates. Reject the optimized generation on any temporal-admissibility, provider-coherence, coal-boundary, schema or target mismatch. Benchmark the same 14-day sample: single-worker throughput must be at least 5x scalar and two-worker aggregate at least 7x; combined RSS must remain at or below 8 GB, free disk at or above 20 GB, with no more than two feature workers or four model threads. Persist throughput, ETA, peak RSS and coverage by partition. No performance result relaxes the statistical acceptance or production-promotion gates.

Revision 1.6 (before provider capability caching): after a hard BOM historical single-run failure in one campaign process, record the failure and skip repeated BOM probes for later dates in that same process, routing them to ECMWF. A restarted process probes BOM once again. This is transport capability caching, not cross-provider imputation: every published weather run still comes wholly from one provider and records the BOM fallback reason. It prevents repeated known-unavailable calls from exhausting the daily site-equivalent quota.

Revision 1.5 (before weather restart reuse): a previously complete weather run is reusable across methodology-only revisions when its normalized and raw hashes match its immutable metadata, its provider/run identity matches the requested run, all 15 sites and required variables are present, and its row count and units pass the current source contract. Re-register those verified artifacts under the current methodology without network access. Parser/schema changes still require an explicit contract-version migration or reacquisition; never reuse a partial group or merely trust file existence.

Revision 1.4 (before weather transport hardening): a coherent weather run may be fetched in deterministic groups of five sites when the 15-site request times out. Every group must use the same provider, initialization, variables, units and forecast horizon; a missing group rejects the entire run. Retry transient timeouts, HTTP 429 and 5xx responses at most three times with bounded Retry-After/exponential waits, recording group and attempt checkpoints. Do not mix providers across groups. API quota accounting remains site-equivalent and failed attempts are retained in the provider audit. Only a complete 15-site response is normalized and published atomically.

The BOM-first availability probe is bounded to one 30-second attempt because the historical single-run provider is known to be intermittently unavailable; ECMWF groups retain three attempts with a 120-second read timeout. This preserves provider priority without multiplying a known provider outage across every historical date.

Revision 1.3 (before resumable campaign orchestration): the manual run command inventories, backfills every eligible original-report archive, prepares coal, acquires daily 00 UTC coherent weather runs for the historical PASA overlap, then constructs and fits both connector tables. Daily weather sampling is an explicit initial research cadence; it is not an intraday-update benchmark. Rate-limit/resource failures preserve work and stop dependent stages, with reports rebuilt in a finally block. Original raw bytes are reusable but normalized results must match a parser contract version. Coverage scans project only publication timestamps. Feature lookups index by delivery to avoid rescanning entire historical archives. No acquisition day-count gate constitutes interval completeness or final acceptance. Incomplete risk, full-horizon and provenance acceptance gates prohibit promotion even when point models have fitted.

Revision 1.1 (before historical parser implementation): stream each nested report into a Parquet writer instead of collecting an entire month in RAM. MT normalized research storage retains delivery days within ten days of each report generation (raw archives remain complete); this covers the seven-day horizon with boundary allowance and reduces irrelevant multi-year data. This avoids silently interpreting midnight date labels as verified interval boundaries.

## Objectives and constraints

Augment the 41-input QNI/VNI network design with forecast fundamentals. Predict signed flow, export/import mean limits and export/import tight limits over 1–336 half-hours. Orient QNI NSW1→QLD1 and VNI VIC1→NSW1. Use per-connector/target/band estimators with shared configurable code. Baseline families are persistence, seasonal persistence, ridge and LightGBM persistence corrections. Model improvement is a result to measure, not a completion assumption.

Use public AEMO and free Open-Meteo research access. Preserve the existing 15 weather sites. Maximum additional campaign storage including scratch and execution files is 50 GB, free-disk reserve 20 GB, two workers, two threads each. Modelling/search budget is 48 wall hours across resumable batches of at most 12 hours, excluding downloads. Report unexecuted jobs explicitly. No scheduler, purchases, remote publication or production activation.

## Execution protocol

Feature construction checkpoints immutable daily partitions keyed by source hashes, feature code, configuration and methodology. Resume reuses only matching partitions. Continuous features are stored as float32 (matching the retained network design), while targets remain float64; missing values remain missing. Final tables are assembled only after each requested day has completed. A source change creates a new partition namespace rather than mixing vintages from an earlier build.

Before implementing a stage, its methodology section must exist. Freeze a content-addressed methodology snapshot on each job. Ledger statuses: planned, ready, running, completed, failed, blocked, skipped. Record dependencies, acceptance criteria, code/config hashes, ownership, heartbeat, start/end, checkpoint, outputs and checksums. Heartbeat every 30 seconds; reconcile dead processes and invalid hashes on resume. SQLite transactions serialize claims and state exports. Completed jobs require existing verified artifacts. Never silently mark blocked or partial work complete. Source failure may block its dependent jobs while independent jobs continue.

## Feature specification

Preserve network history: flow/tight limits at lags 1/48/336, changes, target anchor, delivery calendar/horizon, directional room/gap/candidates/setter age/switches, tightening/relief and quality. Retain the full 41-column network control. New features are generated by versioned blocks, with name/unit/parents/group and source requirements recorded.

For each region generate demand levels/scenario differences; wind/solar UIGF and constrained MW; signed suppression UIGF-capacity and negative-discrepancy flag; suppression shares with denominator max(abs(UIGF),25 MW); residual demand = demand50 - wind - solar under both definitions. Do not subtract rooftop again. Forecast ramps at 0.5/1/3/6 hours require same run/product. Forecast revisions compare the same delivery across earlier admissible runs.

Coal: offered MW, black/brown totals, known-unit coverage, available/registered fraction, station concentration, daily changes, state/recall summaries. Residual demand minus coal availability is a coal-balance proxy, never total reserve or committed synchronous capacity.

Generate all five region levels and all ten pair totals, signed differences (canonical pair order), absolute differences and guarded ratios. Endpoint imbalance is sink-minus-source, explicitly named. Include rest-of-NEM totals. Role-matched weather gradients use temperature/humidity/wind differences; avoid interpreting temperature sums physically.

Interactions: residual-demand imbalance × room; coal shortfall × residual demand; endpoint coincident stress; renewable surplus × opposite deficit and room; suppression × switch gap; solar decline × demand rise; NOS × balance/coal/surplus; forecast change × setter age/pressure. These are hypotheses. No assumed causal signs or monotonic constraints. Preserve interaction parents when selected. Station weather/coal and commitment/inertia effects cannot be claimed from sparse regional proxies.

## Feature reduction before full training

Within training-only chronological splits, compare (a) correlation-group filtering, (b) standardized elastic-net screening with parent hierarchy, (c) shallow-tree group permutation and sequential group elimination. Remove constants, exact duplicates, >80% missing columns unless explicit quality signals, and redundant near-perfect correlations abs(Spearman)>0.98. Fit fills/scales/clips only on training. Keep interaction groups eligible when parents alone have weak scores. Prefer smallest subset within one block-based standard error of best validation MAE. No fixed feature count. Save raw/filtered/selected schemas, parent closure, rejected reasons, inner losses, stability and runtime. Freeze selection before expensive tuning. Outer evaluation never informs selector choice.

## Acceptance

At least 2% matched MAE improvement per replaced cell with paired 7/14-day bootstrap evidence and Holm adjustment. Mean positive capacity overstatement upper non-inferiority bound <=2% relative deterioration (zero reference implies zero margin); recall lower difference bound >=-2 percentage points; held-out false alerts <=3/day jointly per connector. At least eight weekly evaluation blocks and 30 distinct incidents plus power assessment required. All other cells are inconclusive or rejected. Data-reused history is development evidence. No automatic activation. Missing sources route only to separately validated subsets; otherwise unavailable. Flow is not clipped to independent predicted limits.

## Balanced model campaign amendment — 2026-09-19

Replace the exhaustive model search with a frozen two-stage campaign. Discovery uses the PASA/coal information track, two representative chronological folds spanning early and recent/seasonally distinct history, all four existing lead bands, and the `flow`, `export_tight`, and `import_tight` targets. Compare only network, main fundamentals, full interactions, and endpoint recipes. Screen one regularized linear and one shallow boosting candidate per recipe with two VNI-local cell workers. Freeze the recipe, family, hyperparameters and feature set independently by target and lead band using discovery evidence only.

Confirmation compares only the frozen winner and matched network baseline over every mature PASA/coal fold. ECMWF is a sensitivity track on two representative folds using the frozen choices; it is not pooled with PASA/coal or treated as another full search. Ordinary `export` and `import` targets are deferred. Preserve earlier exhaustive-search cells as pilot evidence under their existing contract; never mix them into balanced results.

Final horizon reporting is limited to literal 24-hour, 48-hour, 168-hour and 336-hour delivery horizons, corresponding to half-hour lead indices 48, 96, 336 and 672. The 336-hour horizon lies beyond the seven-day ST PASA and retained weather coverage. Evaluate it only as a separately labelled PD-PASA/coal-only long-range track when an admissible coherent PD vintage and outcome exist; otherwise report it unavailable. Do not impute ST or weather beyond source coverage.
