Section 02 · Missing Valuation Analysis & Analytical Depth Audit

What is missing or too shallow to truly understand and implement valuation

Beyond structure and prose, a line-by-line review of nemic/valuation/*.py and evidence/*.csv uncovered substantive gaps in how valuation works, several high-value results already sitting unused in local CSVs, and four subtle methodological defects in the current valuation scripts.

Code & Methodological Audit · Four Subtle Defects Found in nemic/valuation/
  • In-sample flow-regime peek in the spread-option benchmark (valuation_tests.py lines 110–115): option_benchmark() estimates regime parameters on the prior four quarters, then evaluates out-of-sample payoffs using test.reg, which is the realised future flow-direction regime of the test quarter rather than a prior-quarter regime frequency distribution. This understates out-of-sample error and must be corrected to an ex-ante regime distribution.
  • Undisclosed 4-day sample collapse in the strategic pilot (strategic_pilot_status.json): Four of the six targeted region-months failed with BIDPEROFFER_D not found (NSW May 2024, SA July 2024, QLD Dec 2023 and Jan 2024). Chapter 16 reports "1,152 intervals" without stating that the entire pilot rests on just two calendar days in NSW, two in SA, and zero in QLD.
  • Silent 54% sample drop in walk-forward scoring (valuation_tests.py line 53): Requiring len(hist) >= 4 drops 720 of 1,332 delivered tranches without reporting the remaining auction-date range, and pools all six directions into one unweighted dollar MAE where $28k QLDNSW units swamp $4k NSWVIC units.
  • Unused Cumulative Price Threshold in MPC rescaling (evidence.py lines 182–196): mpc_normalised() loads ADMINISTERED_PRICE_THRESHOLD from AEMO tables and never applies it. Scaling historical cap-binding intervals up to $23,200/MWh linearly without simulating the rolling 7-day CPT breach and $600/MWh administered-price cap overstates the scarcity uplift of higher statutory caps.
