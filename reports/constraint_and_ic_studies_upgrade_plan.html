Section 02 · Root-Cause Diagnosis

Three structural blind spots limiting usability across the constraint and IC studies

Reading the methodology files and results summaries side-by-side exposes three recurring analytical limitations that cut across every single report family above. Fixing these three issues yields far more practical value for a trading or quant workflow than simply polishing prose.

1. The "MW-Without-Dollars" Disconnect

The Six-Link Regime report explicitly declares "prices are outside scope", the three Two-Year Constraint Studies rank generators purely by unweighted sum(|-b/a × ΔMW|) across all 210,240 intervals, and the NOS Outage report stops at MW capacity changes and raw median marginal value ($35.7/MWh).

Operational failure mode: A solar farm ramping 100 MW while VNI has 450 MW of unused headroom and a $0/MWh spread accumulates the same mechanical exposure score as Tumut 3 or Victorian Big Battery tightening a binding constraint during a $5,000/MWh evening spike. None of the constraint studies tell a desk which outages or generators actually create price separation or destroy SRA firmness.

2. Unexplained Error & Calibration Cliffs

Three major quantitative anomalies sit documented in results tables without diagnostic resolution:

  • Reconstruction MAE explosion: VNI reconciles at 7.1 / 22.6 MW (92.1% version match), whereas V-SA jumps 18× to 126.8 / 130.0 MW (79.7%) and QNI jumps 58× to 410.6 / 248.9 MW (65.3%, upper setter match 12%–32%).
  • NOS outlook over-prediction: Beats baseline in only 23 of 96 cells; top predicted bin averages 36% binding vs 15% realised.
  • Line-outage zero displayable tests: 0 of 500 asset-level price tests pass display gates because per-line event counts are tiny (Line 6 has n = 2).

3. Fragmented Templates & Stranded Local Data

The suite currently splits tightly connected grid physics across 18 separate HTML files totaling 126 MB, including three copy-paste 2-year constraint studies and three 20–27 MB Event Atlases with fewer than 700 words of prose each.

Stranded assets already on disk: Full 24-month constraint archives for Basslink, Directlink, and Murraylink already exist under data/constraint_*_2y/, yet none of those three connectors have a 2-year constraint study or contraction attribution. Likewise, observed EnergyConnect commissioning telemetry sits isolated in data/pec_limits_v1/ rather than feeding the AEMC loop valuation model.

Section 03 · Editorial & Engineering Standard

Applying the virtual-barrels-analysis methodology to physical grid reports

Just as we transformed interregional_valuation_research_v2 into Version 3, applying the Virtual Barrels standard to the constraint-pressure and IC studies is not merely stylistic smoothing. It enforces a strict separation between narrative reasoning (which belongs inline in clean prose, equations, and curated charts) and bulk lookup tables (which belong in compact, searchable interactive controls and downloadable CSVs).

Virtual Barrels principle Current defect across constraint / IC reports Target upgraded standard (matching Valuation v3)
Mechanics-first chapter order Reports jump straight into dense summary tables (NOS: 5% prose; Clustering: 3% prose) before explaining how generic constraints, LHS sensitivities, RHS shifts, and binding marginal values physically determine flows and prices. Restructure each flagship report into sequential Parts: (I) Grid & Equation Plumbing, (II) Empirical Constraint & Contraction Structure, (III) Economic / Price / SRA Translation, and (IV) Calibrated Outlook & Modelling Spec.
Narrative-to-table balance & lookup slimming NOS embeds 123,871 words of raw HTML tables; Line Outages embeds 338 tables (100k words); Event Atlases weigh 20–27 MB each from unoptimized inline JSON blobs. Cap inline prose tables to top-10/15 executive summaries (raising prose share to 55%–70%), move full 50-line / 410-family / 230-DUID directories into one paginated client-side searchable lookup + embedded CSV downloads, and compress Event Atlas JSON by ~75% using columnar arrays.
Inline charts & ≤2 consecutive figures Six-Link Regime stacks 6 bare figures; NOS stacks 7 bare figures; Event Atlases stack 3; Forecast Research docs have 0 charts across 24,500 words. Weave every Plotly chart inline inside its analytical subsection, strictly enforce max 2 consecutive exhibits without prose, lead every paragraph with the physical takeaway (zero robotic "Chart X shows"), and render on pure #ffffff white canvases.
Clean domain language & KaTeX math Raw MMS table names (DISPATCHCONSTRAINT, SPDINTERCONNECTORCONSTRAINT, EXPORTLIMIT), raw filenames (nos_binding_asknown.csv), snake_case headers (net_mw, switch_composite_mw), scientific noise (-5.68434e-14), and 3,600+ em-dashes; zero KaTeX math across all 18 files. Relocate all MMS schemas, file lineage, and rebuild commands into Technical Appendix B; render every constraint-envelope, DUID sensitivity (si = −bi/a), and shrinkage formula in server-side KaTeX; enforce automated lint_virtual_barrels.py --strict-emdash with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
Evidence hierarchy & epistemic honesty Strong caveats exist in footnotes, but headline rankings mix mechanical associations, non-binding moves, and uncalibrated outlook probabilities together. Use colour-coded Virtual Barrels callouts (TAKEAWAY, CONTEXT, WARNING) to separate mechanical equation accounting, matched observational contrasts, and calibrated out-of-sample forecasts, backed by automated Playwright desktop/mobile QA.
