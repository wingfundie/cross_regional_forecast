02 · METHODS & LIMITATIONS

Frozen evaluation contract

Daily origins are fixed at 08:00 NEM time. Inputs use one coherent MT PASA run available at the origin. The challenger adds source and sink regional available capacity, positive-capacity unit share, unit count and recall-time summaries to calendar, lead and issue-known target-anchor features.

Partitions are chronological and purge outcomes that have not matured by the next boundary. Model choice uses selection only; residual intervals use calibration only; all published skill and coverage comes from evaluation. Decisions must beat both the seasonal calendar model and target persistence. Seven-day moving blocks preserve short-run dependence in confidence intervals.

The dataset is historical and the source timestamp is a report-generation proxy. Weather, demand, VRE, future dispatch, constraint setters and realised availability are excluded. Prospective received-at collection and revised-outcome scoring remain mandatory.
